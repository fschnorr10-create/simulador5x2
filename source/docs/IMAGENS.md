# Imagens da versão 1.1

## Banner dos setores

Arquivo: `src/assets/setores-banner.webp`. Gerado com a ferramenta integrada imagegen; conversão para WebP com Sharp, sem alteração da composição. Uma faixa fotográfica de quatro painéis, apresentada em recortes CSS, representa os setores. Imagens ilustrativas, sem retratar clientes reais.

Prompt utilizado:

+Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a single editorial website banner composed of four equally sized photographic panels arranged left to right, all panels same width, no gaps, wide landscape 3:1 overall composition. Primary request: represent four main client sectors for a Brazilian employment planning simulator. Panel 1 Brazilian irrigated fruit agriculture: agronomist and farm worker with appropriate sun protection checking grape vines in a green vineyard. Panel 2 industry: two industrial professionals with hard hats and protective eyewear examining a modern clean manufacturing floor from a safe walkway. Panel 3 schools: adult teachers planning lessons around a table in a bright school classroom, no children. Panel 4 charitable institutions: adult community support workers organizing food donation baskets in a welcoming community centre, dignified professional teamwork. Style: premium natural editorial photography, realistic Brazilian diversity, cohesive warm daylight, muted forest green and gold accents, thoughtful professional scenes. Each panel's subjects centered independently so panel crops work individually as well as a whole banner. No text, no logos, no diagrams, no UI, no watermarks. Not actual clients or real identifiable public figures.
